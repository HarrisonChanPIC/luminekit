# Promote and reconcile knowledge

Use when authoring or changing a knowledge owner. Identify subject/entity, applicable period and actual source/revision. Source disagreements must be resolved or visibly qualified before their dependent promotion or disposal.

## Knowledge roles

- **Current knowledge:** state only the supported operating fact or procedure. Source inspection, supplied reports and performed checks have different evidential bases; identify the relevant basis instead of implying deployment or execution.
- **Approved future change:** retain the still-current rule through its period and apply the new rule from its effective date. Approval does not establish that implementation or the effective date has arrived.
- **Proposal:** keep unapproved changes distinct from the operative rule. After verified promotion, reconcile its status or link it as historical basis.
- **Rationale:** retain consequential choices, useful rejected alternatives, consequences and reintroduction conditions in a separate decision/rationale owner linked from the relevant task. Keep the resulting operative constraint in current guidance. A losing local option is not a universal ban.
- **Original evidence:** retain controlled identity, revision and source/export relationships. A newer derivative does not make a received original disposable or defective.
- **Active work and recall:** the existing tracker owns task status; a continuation note carries only otherwise missing unfinished state. Neither establishes current policy or approval.

Combine only material needed for the same reader task, such as a current rule and its approved-future version with clear periods. Follow [content placement](../../luminekit-docs-adopt/references/owners-and-navigation.md#place-content-by-reader-task): contextual history, work logs, run evidence and decision narratives stay outside current guidance. Reuse existing owners instead of creating parallel registers or a prescribed folder system. Unknown, zero, false and not applicable remain distinct values.

## Update affected owners

When updating an index or roadmap, follow [owners and navigation](../../luminekit-docs-adopt/references/owners-and-navigation.md#keep-indexes-useful-as-work-grows): keep its summary/routes concise and put substantive unit plans, rationale and evidence in linked owners. Check this during ordinary progress updates, before another completed pass makes the entry a work chronicle. Follow the project's planning convention for the actual locations.

1. Compare the proposed claim with source evidence and its role/period. Select its focused owner before adding content: reuse the relevant owner, route to another existing owner, or extract a newly independent concern regardless of size. Preserve a contradictory requirement and observation as a named discrepancy; do not change implementation just to make the prose agree.
2. Map affected propositions, routes and derived views. Use [meaning and retrieval](../../luminekit-docs-review/references/meaning-and-retrieval.md#compare-propositions) for complete conditions, rather than relying on matching keywords or revision markers.
3. Apply the bounded owner change under the task's change safeguards, then align affected routes/derivatives in the same unit. A missing export adapter leaves that derivative pending while supported owner work can finish.
4. Read back actual destinations against sources and current/future periods. Use [structure checks](../../luminekit-docs-review/references/structure-and-evidence.md) for links, current derivative correspondence or affected governed records. Deliver the supported result and exact pending conflict/derivative.
