# Choose a useful session boundary

Use when a person/session change or ineffective investigation gives a reason for fresh context.

1. Identify the unfinished objective, latest corrections and next useful boundary. Keep long raw logs/diffs in accessible artifacts and retrieve bounded relevant portions rather than carrying them repeatedly in context.
2. At an agreed boundary, use [capture](capture.md) for otherwise missing recovery state. Existing owners may suffice; preserve active workers and completed valid work. For ineffective approaches, use the task's diagnosis procedure before merely moving an unresolved failure.
3. After the transition, use [recover](resume.md) against current sources. Report recommended, checkpoint prepared and fresh-reader resumption distinctly. A returning author/candidate remains that author/candidate, not an independent reviewer or judge.

A concise resolved result can finish without a session migration.
