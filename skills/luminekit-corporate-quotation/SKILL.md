# Prepare the supported quotation

Use the [luminekit-workflow](../luminekit-workflow/SKILL.md) skill if its instructions are not already loaded. Reuse current task state, then continue within this skill’s scope.

Prepare/revise the offer or return its requested review.

Identify the customer, issuing seller/contracting entity and upstream vendor separately. A supplier cost, internal estimate, proposed selling price and approved customer offer are different bases; a group alias does not choose the contracting entity. Use the record/revision that actually governs each term, rather than borrowing from an unrelated old offer.

Keep one-time, recurring, per-unit, per-site and full-term amounts distinct. Preserve service dates, renewal/validity, payment milestones, deposits and credits, delivery prerequisites and installation/support exclusions. A requested response deadline or tentative schedule is not a delivery guarantee.

Check the selected offer against the approved commercial basis, including entities, scope/items, price/unit/period, currency, tax/rounding, conditions and validity. Use the [artifact procedure](../luminekit-workflow/references/artifacts.md) for independent calculations and actual source/export checks. Keep supplier costs, margins and negotiating rationale outside customer-facing content unless their disclosure is authorized.

An unresolved entity, price, tax basis or commercial decision holds the affected issue-ready claim. For returned corrections, verify the particular issue in the actual revised source/export: a fixed total cannot cure a lost delivery condition, wrong entity or stale attachment. Draft, approved terms, issued offer and sent message are separate states.

For requested email/chat delivery, use the [luminekit-corporate-correspondence](../luminekit-corporate-correspondence/SKILL.md) skill; reuse it if already loaded.
