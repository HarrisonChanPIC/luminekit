# Review the actual contract

Follow the `luminekit-workflow` skill within this skill’s scope.

Establish our side, question/coverage and relevant family/revision/execution status. Use actual instructions/playbook and jurisdictional evidence where needed. Missing policy limits its dependent judgment while literal textual/commercial comparison can proceed.

| Concern | Procedure |
| --- | --- |
| Full/bounded review, NDA scope or material dependencies | [Review scope](references/review-scope.md) |
| Family identity, precedence or approval/execution evidence | Family-and-terms procedure of the `luminekit-contract-draft` skill, without drafting scope |
| Findings, negotiation responses or returned revisions | [Findings and follow-up](references/findings-and-follow-up.md) |
| Formats, redlines, rendering or source/export correspondence | Artifacts procedure of the `luminekit-workflow` skill |

Analyze operative text for the actual party: who owes what, under which timing/conditions/exceptions. Check agreed changes and unexpected changes against the real returned revision; preserve stable finding identities and distinguish accepted replies from implemented text. Do not invent law, standard clauses or company negotiating positions.

Deliver supported findings/proposals/dispositions, material dependencies and next decisions. Keep internal/private reasoning out of counterparty-ready responses. Review-only does not rewrite the agreement; proposed replacement wording is not acceptance or an applied change.

For requested email/chat delivery, use the `luminekit-corporate-correspondence` skill.
