# Choose the contract review scope

For linked sources, precedence, status or denied material dependencies, use the shared [family and terms](../../luminekit-contract-draft/references/family-and-terms.md) procedure. Read it directly; the drafting entry is not needed.

## Full review

Inspect the requested operative family and its material definitions, schedules, exceptions, cross-references, survival and precedence interactions. Use the actual business purpose and applicable supplied playbook or approved positions.

Cover the subjects present: scope/deliverables/acceptance, charges/payment, term/termination, confidentiality/data/IP, liability/remedies and other material obligations. This is a coverage aid, not a requirement to insert every clause. Do not reject a provision merely because it differs from a preferred template. Qualify the full-review claim where required content is unreadable or unavailable.

## Bounded question

Answer the question through only its necessary permitted dependencies. State whether the answer describes current text or a proposal conditional on execution. A payment question may require a definition or precedence clause; it need not expand into unrelated confidentiality analysis. A qualified explanation of visible wording and a specific missing comparison can complete the bounded task.

## NDA screening

Check that the instrument is an NDA; identify unilateral/mutual roles and the permitted purpose. Trace its confidentiality definitions, exclusions, permitted recipients/use, compelled disclosure, duration/survival and return/destruction obligations.

Look for additional business restrictions such as restrictive covenants, exclusivity, IP transfer, broad indemnity or fees. Explain actual wording and supported consequences without imposing default durations, law or arbitration preferences. Expand to the appropriate review scope for a complex or non-NDA instrument; NDA screening is not a full legal verdict or approval to sign.
