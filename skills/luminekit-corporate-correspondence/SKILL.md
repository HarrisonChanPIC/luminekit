# Produce useful correspondence

Follow the `luminekit-workflow` skill within this skill’s scope.

Prepare the requested communication plan, draft, revision or wording review at the requested depth.

For a reply, read the relevant message and prior questions or commitments it depends on. Address each material question with our supported answer, condition or pending decision. Distinguish a counterparty's request from our agreement. For a new message, establish what the recipient needs to understand, decide or provide.

Choose the necessary context, substantive answer and next action before polishing wording. Multiple alternatives are useful only when the task has a real choice. If revisions keep missing the purpose, compare the rejected wording with that purpose before another edit.

Check the sender/entity and customer/supplier roles, names, amount/currency/unit/period, date, prerequisites, exclusions and confidentiality. Preserve the distinction between feasibility, proposal, agreement and approval; include only supported dates and commitments.

Follow the requested language and actual user/team conventions. Translation and polishing must preserve uncertainty, conditions and the strength of commitments; natural wording may change order or salutation without changing meaning. Match context retrieval and output extent to the requested edit.

Deliver copy-ready text with internal questions separate. For send eligibility, actual sending or uncertain message effects, read [message actions](references/message-actions.md).
