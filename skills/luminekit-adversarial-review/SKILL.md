# Challenge the result

Follow the `luminekit-workflow` skill within this skill’s scope.

Collect and verify problems in a scoped artifact against independent requirements. Resolve the subject/revision, affected consumers, applicable domain criteria and consequential failure conditions. The method retains every supported finding rather than choosing a winning reviewer.

1. Select complementary failure questions, bounded probes and benign counterexamples, without a defect quota. Read the independent-runs procedure of the `luminekit-arena` skill before delegating. Keep the caller's review-only effects.
2. Inspect and collect concerns from actual attempts. A synthetic reviewer fixture is supplied evidence, not live fanout. Do not patch the subject or exploit a live target because an issue was raised.
3. Verify every returned concern, including disagreements/late results, against requirements and counterevidence, using the adversarial evidence levels in the `luminekit-workflow` skill. Rerun a returned probe when feasible; check that it reaches the actual claimed violation. A hypothetical future defect or irrelevant failure does not establish the finding.
4. Merge duplicate causes while retaining distinct witnesses/conditions; keep separate causes separate. Resolve contradictions from evidence, not votes/confidence. Preserve material rejected, disputed and unresolved dispositions.
5. Report supported findings with their evidence level and missing coverage at useful priority.

Return scoped no-findings when justified; report material dropped concerns and their reasons.
