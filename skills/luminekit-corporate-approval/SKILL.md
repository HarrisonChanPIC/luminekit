# Prepare the approval case

Use the [luminekit-workflow](../luminekit-workflow/SKILL.md) skill if its instructions are not already loaded. Reuse current task state, then continue within this skill’s scope.

Produce the requested decision case, copy-ready fields or review.

Use the selected current form/revision and owner instructions. Preserve official writable field labels; distinguish observed fields from verified required-field, selector and live validation behavior. Do not invent system-issued numbers, applicant hierarchy, approval history, signatures or status. A historical form print can support a bounded copy-ready example, not prove today's portal schema.

Frame the actual decision through relevant background/need, proposed action, alternatives or procurement basis, expected outcome and material cost/risk. These are argument roles rather than mandatory headings. Resolve entity, budget/code owner, classification, routing and approver from applicable current sources; historical consent and sample checkbox choices do not select them.

Use the decision's financial basis. Supplier expenditure, customer revenue, internal allocation and payable totals are distinct; procurement/expense requests use expenditure rather than sales revenue. Proposed budget, available funding and an approved commitment are separate claims. Apply the [artifact procedure](../luminekit-workflow/references/artifacts.md) to calculations and attachment/source correspondence.

Review actual fields and attachments for unsupported amounts/authority, omitted decision facts, wrong roles/routing, lost conditions and source/export mismatches. Retain exact field identities in the requested language. Packet readiness, form validity, authority, attempted submission and confirmed workflow effect are distinct states; submission requires the actual account, form/revision, fields, attachments, target and permitted effect to match its approval.

For requested email/chat delivery, use the [luminekit-corporate-correspondence](../luminekit-corporate-correspondence/SKILL.md) skill; reuse it if already loaded.
