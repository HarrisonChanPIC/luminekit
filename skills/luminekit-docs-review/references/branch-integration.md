# Compare promises across revisions

Use for a proposal/target or available branch comparison. Different files can make incompatible promises without a text merge conflict; distinct audiences, contracts or effective periods can legitimately differ.

1. Identify actual proposal/target revisions, relevant dirty bytes/configuration and whether each is an available checkout or supplied snapshot. A proposal-only scan cannot establish integration compatibility. Name unseen branches and unavailable incorporated sources.
2. Read affected owners and known incoming routes on both sides. Use [meaning comparison](meaning-and-retrieval.md#compare-propositions) for overlapping promises and [knowledge roles](../../luminekit-docs-maintain/references/owner-updates.md#knowledge-roles) for approval, current/proposed/history and period distinctions. An explicit superseding change can legitimately differ; identify unresolved coexistence rather than treating every change as a defect.
3. Follow existing entries as a reader would after integration. Flag obsolete, conflicting or incomplete reachable guidance, including unchanged incoming pointers. Use [structure checks](structure-and-evidence.md) for mechanical/declared-rule claims.
4. Report exact competing passages/identities, their shared or differing scope, consequence and correction at the actual owner. Accept valid scoped differences. A required business decision remains with its actual authority.
5. Before an integration-readiness conclusion, recheck relevant proposal/target and dirty/configuration identities. Changed evidence invalidates affected conclusions, leaving unrelated evidence usable. Immutable supplied snapshots establish only their own comparison; unavailable live freshness cannot support a current-target verdict.

Deliver findings or a bounded no-findings result, valid keeps and exact comparison/check limits. Use available local/CI checks within their observed capabilities; identify actual tool/config/input/output and missing coverage. A clean merge or deterministic structural pass does not establish semantic compatibility or adopted enforcement.
