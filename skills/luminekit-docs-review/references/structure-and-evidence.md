# Check structure, currentness and governed records

Read only the owners/dependencies required by the selected reader task. Unclear ownership matters where it creates contradictory guidance, update risk or retrieval failure. Navigation should point to the rule's owner rather than repeat it. Combined material must serve the same reader task, with roles and effective periods clear.

## Disclosure and ownership

Apply [content placement](../../luminekit-docs-adopt/references/owners-and-navigation.md#place-content-by-reader-task) regardless of document size. Check for substantive task details embedded in entries, independently needed concerns in one compulsory read unit, duplicated rules, and contextual history or work evidence in current guidance. A heading link into the same compulsory monolith does not establish extraction. Also check for fragmented procedures, empty navigation layers and unnecessary hops.

Follow the selected task's branch and required dependencies rather than prefetching every link. Current guidance must work without historical originals or ignored work files. An exception, prerequisite or recovery step needed for the action belongs at the point of use or behind an explicit required dependency; moving it out of sight is not a disclosure improvement. Classifying operative guidance as evidence does not excuse a hidden dependency.

For each finding identify the passage, affected reader task, unnecessary reading/update risk or missing condition, and bounded correction. Length alone is not a defect. Preserve justified coherent procedures and adequate routes; use [meaning comparison](meaning-and-retrieval.md#compare-propositions) to verify that extraction preserves complete rules and explicit discrepancies.

## Links and current derivatives

Resolve local destinations relative to their source and inspect the target or heading. For a parsed-link claim use the actual Markdown dialect/parser, including encoded or angle-bracket paths with spaces. Manual destination/heading checks are useful within their narrower scope. Denied remote sources, ambiguous anchors and unseen incoming routes remain qualified gaps rather than proven missing files.

For an output declared current, compare its source identity/revision with the current authoring source and check actual meaning. A revision mismatch establishes stale declared correspondence; a match alone does not establish fidelity. Use [knowledge lifecycle](../../luminekit-docs-maintain/references/owner-updates.md#knowledge-roles) to distinguish requirements, observations, proposals, historical originals and current derivatives. Use [branch integration](branch-integration.md) when proposal/target coexistence is the concern.

## Baselines and exceptions

Match a legacy defect to its path, rule, concrete failure witness and relevant affected input, such as link target and proposition. Equal counts or a reused path/rule slot cannot excuse another defect. An unrelated heading edit need not invalidate the same witness solely because a whole-file hash changed. Report the recorded reason, accountable owner and review condition; missing fields remain unknown.

Check an exception's actual rule/scope, owner, rationale, compensating measure and review/expiry condition. Honor a valid unchanged allowance; enlarged scope, a changed proposition or a concretely expired adopted allowance requires its stated review. A review date does not grant deletion authority. Without adopted blocking/expiry behavior, report the observed error, warning or incomplete state rather than invent policy.

This procedure assesses the record. If its update/removal is also authorized, use [owner updates](../../luminekit-docs-maintain/references/owner-updates.md#update-affected-owners), recheck the actual affected witness and retain resolution evidence. Do not silently rebaseline a new defect or widen an allowance to pass. Structural success remains separate from semantic correctness and adopted enforcement.
