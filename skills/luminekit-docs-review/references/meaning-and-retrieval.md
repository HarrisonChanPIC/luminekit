# Compare complete meaning and the reader route

Use to verify revised/split knowledge or whether a reader can recover an operative rule. Identify authoritative source and result versions, relevant reader task, entity, role and period. A pre-existing ambiguity differs from a newly lost obligation. Source roles and currentness follow [knowledge lifecycle](../../luminekit-docs-maintain/references/owner-updates.md#knowledge-roles).

## Compare propositions

Map each relevant proposition to the reached owner: actor/action, trigger/scope, timing/order, units/periods, must/may/never, exceptions/negative guarantees, ownership, side effects, failure and consequence. Keep each condition with its action. Preserve still-current rules while adding future changes; a received original is not superseded merely by a new derivative.

Read the actual procedure and its necessary dependencies. A permission must not become a mandate or prohibition, and an accurate root summary cannot repair an incomplete instruction at the point of use. Accept equivalent wording and coherent combined files. Include unique useful rationale and source/revision pointers. In new/sparse adoption, verify that useful supported knowledge was authored rather than only headings or navigation.

Try the selected task and a meaningful boundary/non-trigger or conditional-exception question. Report each lost/changed proposition, its passage, reader consequence and bounded correction. Missing incorporated content limits its dependent preservation conclusion; a working link or keyword match cannot fill that gap. Use [structure checks](structure-and-evidence.md) for destinations/anchors and [branch integration](branch-integration.md) for proposal/target compatibility.

## Observe retrieval when needed

For a meaningful adoption or a claimed retrieval result, an authorized fresh reader gets only the final permitted workspace, actual entry and natural task. Withhold expected answers, the migration response, before-corpus and unrelated private context. Observe actual owners read and the resulting answer against independent source propositions. Use separate contexts when branch inputs would leak into later questions.

A walkthrough remains a walkthrough; unavailable fresh-reader access stays unverified. Actual probes identify their inputs, read resources and outputs. Keep semantic preservation, structural integrity, retrieval success and automatic host loading as distinct results. A source-linked defect can be useful without execution, while no findings in the selected subset cannot certify unseen scope.
