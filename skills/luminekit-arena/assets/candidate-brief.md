# Candidate brief

Fill the fields needed for this task; a pointer must be accessible in the recipient's actual context. Share the same controlling basis with every candidate, changing only assigned scope and output destination.

- **Product and purpose:** the bounded question and requested artifact; independent recommendation or design exploration.
- **Required facts and constraints:** established requirements, accepted decisions and limits every feasible answer must satisfy.
- **Open choices:** decisions candidates may vary, including the mechanism being explored.
- **Source identities and task grounding:** relevant paths/revisions, inputs, contracts and authorized background; disclose missing or different access.
- **Public acceptance:** observable outcomes and failure conditions; distinguish these from private evaluator answers.
- **Permitted effects:** inspection, probes or writes actually authorized, and protected inputs.
- **Output destination:** this candidate's isolated writable location, or reply-only output.
- **Stopping budget:** bounded attempts, time or probe scope, and what to return if blocked.
- **Return:** coherent mechanism, evidence against acceptance, alternatives rejected and why, assumptions and unperformed checks.

Keep the parent's preferred design, conversational reasoning, sibling outputs and private evaluator answers out of the dispatch. Record the dispatched brief plus actual fresh/inherited context and known model identity alongside collected results.
