# Independent work: frame, isolate and collect

This is the shared execution procedure for independent candidate, reviewer and judge contexts. The caller's method owns what to ask and how to assess the results. Serial self-generated alternatives and fixed responses can be useful supplied material, but do not establish independent work.

## Before dispatch

Fix the subject/question, relevant source/revision identity, controlling facts, constraints, public criteria, permitted effects and a stopping budget before outputs. Reuse an adequate brief. Give contexts equivalent authorized grounding and disclose material access differences; do not copy denied sources to equalize them.

Choose roles from actual capabilities. Use separate contexts when claiming independence and state sequential versus parallel execution accurately. Sessions alone do not prove different models; record model/provider identity only when known. Keep sibling outputs and private evaluator answers out of independent participant inputs. Do not prime a preferred answer or suspected defect unless explicitly verifying that finding.

Ask each candidate for a coherent approach and criterion support, or each reviewer for source-supported findings and limits or scoped no findings. Bound any complementary scopes and output locations. A packet does not create tools, isolation, access or authority the context lacks.

## Collect and hand back

Preserve actual output/context identities and inspected scope. Distinguish completed, partial, failed, timed out, refused, truncated, cancelled and pending work; silence/dropout is missing coverage, not a vote or clean review. Collect late results or disclose them as pending before closing the selected scope. A supplied response remains supplied evidence.

Retry or replace only within the actual task budget, keeping the relevant brief and criteria comparable. Disclose input changes and reevaluate affected results when criteria are legitimately corrected. Without usable independent capability, return supported work with its reduced claim instead of inventing contexts or spinning in tool calls.

A separate judge receives the same controlling basis, exact actual outputs and missing-coverage information, without evaluator answers or a preferred verdict. Otherwise label controller review. Return the collected evidence and actual role/model/execution limits to the caller's comparison or finding-verification procedure.
