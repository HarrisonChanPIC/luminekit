# Architecture sketch

Use within the requested plan or reply; adapt the sections to the actual decision.

## Basis

Affected code/callers and source identities; fixed contracts and recorded rationale; open choices and independent acceptance.

## Caller usage

Representative usage plus two or three meaningful call sites, including a boundary or failure case. Show inputs, results and coordination before deriving the shape.

## Candidate shapes

For each distinct mechanism: core representation, signatures, module responsibilities, state/effect owners and relevant failure/lifetime rules. Trace the same callers through it. Show where invariants live and what consumers must know.

## Decision

Chosen base and source-supported reasons; compatible contributions and rejected alternatives; consumer consequences and accepted tradeoffs. Preserve actual Arena coverage and limits.

## Implementation and verification needs

Affected units and dependencies; unresolved product decisions; independent observations needed to confirm the sketch. Distinguish planned checks from performed evidence.
