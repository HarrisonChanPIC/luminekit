# Draft the requested instrument

Use the applicable branch after resolving the necessary family and authorized terms.

## Agreement or template filling

For an approved template with complete variables, replace only the supplied fields and verify every intended replacement; leave operative clauses intact. For substantive drafting, build the required definitions, obligations and schedules from the supplied terms. Retain controlled clauses unless their changes are authorized. Check obligations introduced by schedules and coherent cross-references.

## Statement of work

Identify the exact governing agreement and new SOW. Include applicable supplied site/service, deliverables, responsibilities, acceptance criteria, scope exclusions, quantity, term, fees and payment dependencies. Do not enlarge a small service SOW with invented milestones or an acceptance regime.

Follow the governing instrument's inheritance and precedence. A new SOW does not amend another SOW or override the Master by implication. Check references, definitions and dates together with the commercial scope.

## Amendment

Identify the baseline instrument/revision and actual amendment requirements. State each authorized change using unambiguous clause/location identification and replacement or addition text. Apply it only from the instructed effective date; preserve prior periods and unaffected surviving terms. A general surviving-terms statement must not conceal a conflicting change.

Keep proposed effect distinct from source execution status. Do not invent retroactivity, proration or an override of the governing instrument. If instructions conflict with the amendment mechanism, identify that issue and prepare supported proposed wording without claiming legal effect.

## Requested revision

Start from the identified working revision and create the requested controlled derivative. Include only authorized substance and necessary connected definition/reference changes, identifying consequential edits separately from unrelated improvements. A revised draft does not itself amend an executed instrument.

Compare intended changes, inherited obligations and unexpected differences against the baseline. Drafting self-check does not establish independent legal review or acceptance; actual returned-finding dispositions use the review procedure.
