# Establish the contract family and terms

Use for linked instruments, source/approval conflicts, template suitability or material unknowns in drafting or review. A complete single template can be filled without a family investigation.

1. Identify the actual parties/our side, document IDs/revisions and necessary Master, SOW, amendments, schedules and incorporated terms. Read the relevant content; a summary or attachment name cannot replace an operative dependency.
2. Establish executed/effective, approved-for-drafting, proposed, superseded or unknown status from execution records and actual provisions. A signature block, timestamp or negotiating approval does not prove execution. Follow the actual precedence/amendment mechanism; a later SOW or unsigned override does not automatically change the Master.
3. Keep available, denied, incomplete and absent sources distinct. A manifest proves existence only. Do not reconstruct denied content from another task's memory or copy it into this reader's context. Explain readable proposed text while naming the unavailable baseline or precedence conclusion.
4. Resolve the applicable template/playbook, approved variables, allowed clause changes and actual commercial instructions. A counterparty request is not our concession. If a template is unsuitable, identify the mismatch and the necessary decision; do not substitute stock legal positions, venue, tax rules or approval thresholds.
5. Separate authorized changes from inherited terms and identify their effective period. Capture supplied service/site/quantity, obligations, fees/currency/basis, dates, conditions and exclusions. An undecided material term stays visibly open; do not borrow an old fee as if newly agreed or invent deposits, proration, renewal or liability provisions.

Carry the relevant source/revision basis and open decisions into the requested draft or review. This needs no new contract register or copied correspondence history.
