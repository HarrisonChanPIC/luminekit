# Choose owners and navigation

Start with a representative reader outcome, such as locating setup steps, changing one feature or finding current commercial terms. Follow the existing route before designing another. Use [source-backed authoring](source-backed-authoring.md) only for genuinely missing knowledge; a correct and discoverable route can remain unchanged.

Give each operative proposition one current owner. A route states when to open its target and what it owns rather than copying the rule. Retain prerequisites, exceptions and recovery with the action they qualify. Choose coherent tasks or conditional branches as read units; fixed line counts and one-file-per-sentence structures can fragment meaning.

Reuse established locations, titles and naming conventions. Otherwise choose a small permitted entry and useful owners. Add a directory index only when it reduces navigation work. Keep official identifiers, revisions and original/export lineage; reorganizing guidance is not a reason to bulk-rename received records.

## Keep indexes useful as work grows

An entry or index owns orientation, brief status, shared prerequisites and routes. When a unit's substantive plan, design rationale or acceptance evidence would turn that entry into a detailed work record, move the detail to a focused linked owner in the same update. After completion, update the index summary/link and keep the actual evidence with its unit. A list of headings inside one growing file does not provide progressive disclosure.

Keep order, dependencies, exit criteria and the next action discoverable from the index. Separate a substantial decision comparison or shared preparation record when it serves a distinct reader task; do not repeat it across owners. Use project naming/location conventions and preserve incoming links, complete conditions and useful rationale during the move. A short coherent plan needs no extra index or files, and future units need no empty placeholders.

## Migrate and verify the route

When moving guidance, map useful propositions and known incoming routes before shortening the old entry. Current instructions should reach their owners without requiring historical originals; preserve those originals and useful rationale in their controlled locations. [Migration/application](migration-and-application.md) handles actual moves/replacements.

Read the resulting entry and required destinations in the actual host/link surface, including spaces and anchors. Use [structure checks](../../luminekit-docs-review/references/structure-and-evidence.md) for link/owner defects and [reading cost](../../luminekit-docs-review/references/reading-cost.md) when claiming a reduction. A small owner/route table is sufficient unless repeated use demonstrates a registration need. File existence does not establish automatic host loading.
