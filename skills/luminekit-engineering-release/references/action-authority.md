# Engineering action authority

Use before an actual engineering mutation or retry. Resolve available tools/access, operation, targets, current revision/payload, prerequisites and authority. Missing capability holds the action while preparation continues; do not invent provider syntax or broaden a grant.

Require explicit approval for the actual operation, target, revision and effects. A direct instruction can provide it; unchanged valid authority needs no generic repeat confirmation or email-style later-message ceremony. Preserve exclusions and retry limits. Commits, remote push/PR/merge, ADO comments/fields/state/relations, deployment and migrations have distinct effects.

Recheck the current target and relevant source/check inputs against the grant immediately before acting. A changed head or payload is not covered by approval of its old version. Hold only the dependent operation rather than resetting work or manufacturing a commit.

An engineering retry needs a scoped retry grant plus **conclusive nonexecution or a verified applicable replay guarantee**. Verify the guarantee's actual operation/target/payload coverage; a client ID, identical comment text or command name is not a general deduplication key. Retry only the permitted item, preserving separately confirmed operations. Authority never follows from a provider guarantee alone.

Before attempting or reconciling an operation, use the workflow’s [operation outcomes](../../luminekit-workflow/references/action-outcomes.md).
